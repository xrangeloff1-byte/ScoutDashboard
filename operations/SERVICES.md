## Scout lead research and qualification
1. Find businesses with publicly observable issues; record the URL and evidence, avoiding fabricated problems.
2. Match the specific issue to a feasible service and prepare a concise, personalized draft.
3. Score leads for issue severity, fit, accessibility, and realistic ability to deliver.
4. Obtain owner approval for the target, scope, price, and outreach before sending.
5. Log contact status, opt-outs, replies, proposals, confirmed payments, and delivery status.
6. Never claim a fix was completed without verification or count proposals as revenue.

## Delivery and safety
- Work only on sites/accounts with explicit authorization and suitable access.
- Do not promise security, legal compliance, SEO rankings, revenue, or outcomes that have not been tested.
- Use secure delegated credentials; never store passwords, OAuth tokens, or API keys in this public repository.
- Respect applicable outreach laws, sender identification, opt-outs, and rate limits.
- Use separate business email credentials for Scout's GitHub runtime; ChatGPT Gmail access alone does not authorize GitHub Actions to send mail.
- Keep Beatrice and all its repositories untouched.
