# Cairnflow Private — Launch Operations

**Status:** Pre-launch. No clients, payments, or outreach are claimed.

## Product to validate
AI Inquiry Desk for home-service businesses. Uses business-approved FAQs, business hours, service area, and contact procedures to draft answers, capture inquiries, and route requests to a human. Must not invent pricing, appointments, policies, or warranties.

## Scout's workflow
1. Research businesses using publicly accessible information, record evidence URLs and dates.
2. Score prospects based on observable operational issues; don't infer willingness to buy or actual lost revenue.
3. Draft individual, honest business emails for approval. Respect opt-outs; don't impersonate humans or fabricate relationships.
4. Classify incoming replies and suggest next steps; stop follow-ups for opt-outs.
5. Prepare a scope of work and quote; require approval for binding commitments.
6. Upon approved agreement and confirmed payment, onboard client using only authorized information.
7. Deploy the agreed service in a test environment, then obtain acceptance before public launch.
8. Monitor service quality, expenses, and renewal status.
