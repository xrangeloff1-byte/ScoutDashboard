# Cairnflow Private — Brand Blueprint

**Status:** Proposed brand identity. Not a registered company, trademark, or verified available domain.

**Positioning:** A private, high-touch AI systems atelier offering carefully scoped automation for service businesses.

**Tagline:** Quietly intelligent. Exceptionally efficient.

**What we sell:** Setup and maintenance of customer-inquiry assistants, FAQ automation, lead routing, and appointment-intake systems for small service businesses. We do not promise guaranteed customer revenue.

**First offer:** AI Inquiry Desk — a website assistant that answers approved common questions, collects customer inquiries, and passes them to a human owner.

**Illustrative launch pricing (subject to validation):** $950 setup + $295/month ongoing support. Quote custom integrations separately; do not send invoices or charge cards until fulfillment is verified.

**Scout's role:** Public lead research, evidence-backed qualification, personalized email drafts, reply classification, proposal preparation, onboarding checklists, and billing-status reports.

**Owner approval required before:** outbound sending, contractual commitments, pricing exceptions, payment collection, or deployment into a client account. Follow applicable anti-spam rules and honor opt-outs.

**Tone:** Professional, concise, helpful, transparent that automation is used.

**Brand colors:** obsidian #0B130E, ivory #F3EAD7, champagne gold #C9A96A.

**Sales workflow:** Discover → independently verify → human approve target/offer → send compliant outreach → handle reply → confirm scope → send agreement + payment link via approved provider → deliver tested service → monitor support + retention.

**Next requirements:** business email/domain, legally accurate sender details, payment provider, client-service template, privacy policy, outreach approval process. Domain and trademark availability must be checked before public launch.

## AI-operated business model
- Scout researches prospects, scores evidence, prepares compliant personalized outreach drafts and maintains a CRM.
- AI inbox assistant classifies replies and proposes follow-ups; outgoing sales messages require an authorized email connection and approved sending policy.
- AI proposals use approved service scopes and pricing; contracts, refunds, and unusual commitments require owner sign-off.
- AI delivery prepares client FAQ, intake workflows, tests, and monthly performance reports. Access to customer systems requires explicit authorization.
- Billing integrates with an approved payment provider only after verification; never handle raw card details.
- Profit dashboard tracks actual collected payments less payment fees, service delivery costs, hosting, AI API usage, and refunds. Never count proposals as revenue.
- This is a target operating model, not a claim of clients, revenue, or hands-free production readiness.
