# Cairnflow Studio — Brand Blueprint

**Status:** Proposed brand identity. Not a registered company, trademark, or verified available domain.

**Positioning:** Calm, practical AI automation for service businesses.

**Tagline:** Fewer missed inquiries. More time for the work.

**What we sell:** Setup and maintenance of customer-inquiry assistants, FAQ automation, lead routing, and appointment-intake systems for small service businesses. We do not promise guaranteed customer revenue.

**First offer:** AI Inquiry Desk — a website assistant that answers approved common questions, collects customer inquiries, and passes them to a human owner.

**Illustrative launch pricing (subject to validation):** $399 setup + $129/month ongoing support. Quote custom integrations separately; do not send invoices or charge cards until fulfillment is verified.

**Scout's role:** Public lead research, evidence-backed qualification, personalized email drafts, reply classification, proposal preparation, onboarding checklists, and billing-status reports.

**Owner approval required before:** outbound sending, contractual commitments, pricing exceptions, payment collection, or deployment into a client account. Follow applicable anti-spam rules and honor opt-outs.

**Tone:** Professional, concise, helpful, transparent that automation is used.

**Brand colors:** forest #102017, ivory #F3EAD7, brass #D2AD62.

**Sales workflow:** Discover → independently verify → human approve target/offer → send compliant outreach → handle reply → confirm scope → send agreement + payment link via approved provider → deliver tested service → monitor support + retention.

**Next requirements:** business email/domain, legally accurate sender details, payment provider, client-service template, privacy policy, outreach approval process. Domain and trademark availability must be checked before public launch.
